```text

Primary request: Invent a distinctive FOLDED RESEARCH PAPER WHALE. The forward head is a blunt angular polygon, the body is short and diamond-shaped; its tail folds upward BEHIND the body like an open two-page book and two small crisp folded pectoral fins open outward. This must read as an elegant folded-paper whale and a research manuscript at once. Two tiny dark horizontal eye marks on the front fold and an understated confident expression; very little face detail.
Style: premium editorial paper sculpture photographed as a sophisticated brand mascot, precise geometric folds, broad simple planes, uncoated matte paper, very restrained realistic soft studio light. Strong original silhouette, adult professional charm. Medium-complexity sculpture that also works as a simple icon.
Palette: bauhinia purple #A02070, rich plum #731650, pale mauve #E5BED5, warm ivory interior paper #FFF9F4. No blues, no cyan.
Composition: single complete character centered in a square, occupying about 78 percent of the canvas with clear padding; body is upright rather than a horizontal fish, both eyes visible, broad fluke folds above and behind.
Background: genuine transparent alpha, no stage, no environment, no ground shadow.
Avoid: conventional blue whale profile, round side-facing bubble whale, giant single eye, monocle, spectacles, magnifier, white contrasting belly patch, water spray, coins, stock charts, gradients painted on paper, text, letters, watermark, other props.
```

The visual must be understood first as a distinctive purple flower with a clever whale-tail reference, not as a literal swimming animal. No head, no eyes, no face, no full whale body.

Professional institutional research brand. Strong readable silhouette at 24 pixels. No container, no outlines, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no shadow.
Palette: bauhinia purple #A02070 as dominant, rich plum #731650 and a restrained dusty mauve #C86BA6. Use negative transparent space between petals, not a white box.
Composition: exactly one complete compact emblem, centered with even 15 percent margin on all sides, square canvas. Deliberate proportions, natural vertical elegance rather than a radial corporate pinwheel.
Background: actual fully transparent alpha.
Avoid: generic five-point star, asterisk, lotus, lettermark, literal Chinese characters, coin, shield, blue whale silhouette, magnifying glass, stock-market arrows, typography, mockups, sheet of multiple logos, watermark.
